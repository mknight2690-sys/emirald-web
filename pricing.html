Pricing FAQ

Common questions about our pricing plans

Can I change plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll be charged the prorated difference immediately. When downgrading, the change will take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a contract or commitment?

No! All our plans are month-to-month with no long-term commitments. You can cancel anytime without penalties.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and through Stripe's secure payment processing. We do not store your payment information.

Do you offer discounts for annual billing?

Yes! Annual subscribers get 2 months free. That's a 17% discount compared to monthly billing. You'll see the option during checkout.

What happens if I exceed my plan limits?

If you're on a Starter plan and want to add more trading pairs, you'll be prompted to upgrade to Pro. The system will automatically handle the upgrade and prorated billing.

Can I get a refund?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for new customers. If you're not satisfied within the first 14 days, contact our support team for a full refund.
