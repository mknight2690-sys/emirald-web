Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Emirald

How does Emirald make trading decisions?

Emirald uses advanced AI models to analyze market conditions, price patterns, and trading indicators every 60 seconds. It considers multiple factors including market sentiment, technical analysis, and risk management parameters to make informed trading decisions.

Is my money safe with Emirald?

Yes! Emirald never holds your funds. It only connects to your BloFin account via API keys with read-only and trading permissions. All funds remain in your exchange account. We also implement automatic stop-loss to protect your capital.

What is the free trial period?

We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all Pro features. No credit card required to start. You can test Emirald with paper trading mode before risking real capital.

Which exchanges does Emirald support?

Currently, Emirald supports BloFin for USDT-M Futures trading. We're planning to add more exchanges in future updates based on user demand.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes! You can pause or cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard. There are no cancellation fees. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period.

Do I need to be an experienced trader?

No! Emirald is designed for both beginners and experienced traders. The AI handles all the complex decisions. However, we recommend understanding basic crypto trading concepts before starting.
